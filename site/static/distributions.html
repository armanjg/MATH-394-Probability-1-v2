Hypergeometric

\(w,b,n\)

\(P(X=k)=\dfrac {\binom {w}{k}\binom {b}{n-k}}{\binom {w+b}{n}}\)

\(\displaystyle \sum _{k=0}^{b} e^{tk} \dfrac {\binom {r+k-1}{r-1}\binom {w+b-r-k}{w-r}}{\binom {w+b}{w}}\)
